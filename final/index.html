Enter Your Phone Number

We just need ten digits. How hard could it be?

How it works

  1. Use the arrow buttons to slide a row or column. Tiles that slide off one edge come back on the other.
  2. Get the digit you want into the center tile, which has the red border.
  3. Press Lock in to add that digit to your phone number.
  4. Repeat until all ten digits are filled, then press Submit.
